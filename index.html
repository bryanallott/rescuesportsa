Our Vision

"To be the recognised South African authority for World Rescue Sport, developing athletes who compete with excellence in pool rescue sport and surf rescue sport, while upholding international standards and South African sporting values."

Our Mission

RSSA exists to govern, develop, and promote World Rescue Sport in South Africa, in alignment with International Life Saving (ILS) rules.

  • Administer Pool & Surf disciplines
  • Adapt ILS Technical Rules to SA context
  • Transparent governance & safeguarding

The Disciplines

Pool Rescue Sport

Precision, speed, and manikin handling under ILS standards.

Surf Rescue Sport

Ocean mastery, beach sprints, and board/ski technical skills.

Transparent Governance

RSSA preserves the identity and development priorities of South African rescue sport while remaining fully aligned with the global World Rescue Sport community. We coordinate national championships, rankings, and selection processes with absolute transparency.
